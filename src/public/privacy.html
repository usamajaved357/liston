Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Liston ("we", "our", "the Service") helps sellers draft and manage marketplace listings, including by connecting to third-party platforms such as eBay. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is protected.

Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Connected marketplace credentials: OAuth tokens (e.g. from "Login with eBay") or API credentials you provide to link a marketplace account. These are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and are never stored or logged in plain text.
  • Listing and product data: content you submit or that we generate on your behalf (titles, descriptions, images, prices, categories) in order to create and publish listings to your connected marketplace account.
  • Usage data: basic operational logs (timestamps, request paths) used for debugging and reliability. We do not log request bodies, passwords, or credentials.

How we use information

  • To authenticate you and operate your account.
  • To connect to marketplaces on your behalf (e.g. eBay's Sell APIs) using your own connected credentials, strictly to perform actions you request (drafting, publishing, and managing your own listings).
  • To generate listing content using AI processing, where the inputs are limited to product information relevant to the listing being created.
  • To maintain and improve the Service's reliability and security.

Marketplace account data

Where Liston connects to a marketplace such as eBay via OAuth, we access only the data and permissions necessary to draft, publish, and manage listings on your behalf, as authorized by the scopes you grant during that connection. We do not access or store your marketplace account password. You may revoke this access at any time from your marketplace account's application-permissions settings, or by disconnecting the account within Liston.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share information only:

  • With the marketplace platforms you explicitly connect (e.g. eBay), to perform the actions you request.
  • With service providers that process data on our behalf (e.g. AI content generation, image processing), solely to provide the Service's functionality.
  • Where required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account and listing data for as long as your account is active. If a connected marketplace account is deleted or closed by its owner, and that platform notifies us, we process that notification and do not retain marketplace-account-linked personal data beyond what is needed to comply with that request. You may request deletion of your Liston account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data, including encryption of stored platform credentials, hashed passwords, and access controls limiting who can view your data.
